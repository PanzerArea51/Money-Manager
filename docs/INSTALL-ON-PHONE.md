# Install on your phone

Site address: https://panzerarea51.github.io/Money-Manager/

The app installs to your home screen and opens full screen like a normal app.

## Before you start

Open the site address once while you are online. The app saves itself to your phone on that first visit. After that it opens without internet.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open the site address in Chrome.
2. If an install prompt appears, tap **Install**.
3. If no prompt appears, tap the Chrome menu (three dots) and choose **Install app**.
4. Open the app from its new home screen icon.

## iPhone (Safari)

1. Open the site address in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
4. Open the app from the new home screen icon. This is the only way to get the full-screen (standalone) view. Opening the site in Safari shows it in a normal browser tab.

## Pick one place to enter data

On iPhone, the app in a Safari tab and the app on the home screen can keep separate data. Choose one, ideally the home screen icon, and enter everything there. If you add data in one and then open the other, it looks empty.

## Updating the app

When a new version is ready, a banner says "A new version is available." with two buttons:

- **Update**: applies the new version and reloads the app once. Your data is kept.
- **Later**: keeps the current version. The update applies the next time you fully close and reopen the app.

You need an internet connection to receive an update.

## Your data

- Data is stored only on your phone, in the browser or installed app you used. It is per-device and per-browser. The installed app, the same site in a browser tab, other browsers and other devices do not share data.
- There is no sync and no backup in this release.
- Clearing site data or uninstalling the app can delete your data.
- A JSON export/import backup is planned for later.
